# Iff logo exploration

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Four raster concept presentations; no logo has been applied to the app. A selected concept should be refined as vector artwork and tested at small sizes before production use.

## Prompt directions

Common brief: logo-brand exploration for global app Iff, connecting scattered records to a trusted person's practical handover. One spacious landscape artboard per concept with a large primary logo lockup, a smaller standalone symbol beneath, precise kerning and flat vector-like geometry. Exact lowercase name iff. No marketing tagline, mockup objects, shadows, gradients, texture, 3D or watermark.

1. Shared stem: bespoke soft geometric lowercase iff, a shared crossbar connecting both f letters and an amber i dot. Forest #223e34, amber #986123, ivory #f8f7f3. Include small rounded-square app icon.
2. The thread: continuous bold ribbon forming two supported adjacent open loops, hinting at two f letters and connected lives. Pair with contemporary humanist sans-serif iff. Forest on ivory; avoid a generic infinity sign.
3. Folded note: folded page formed from interlocking broad strokes with negative-space ff; precise geometry and softened corners. Editorial serif iff. Deep ink #263a36 on ivory, restrained terracotta #b37050 accent.
4. Passed forward: two offset rounded rectangular links with a central bridge, abstract handover. Wide rounded sans-serif iff. Restrained cobalt #354f9c on ivory; avoid hearts, hands, shields and generic chain clip art.

## Initial assessment

01 is the clearest, warmest wordmark. 03 has the strongest connection to preserved information and a distinctive standalone mark. 02 is expressive but could be read as an unrelated abstract shape. 04 is clean but its link motif is less distinctive. These are visual explorations, not trademark-cleared identities.
