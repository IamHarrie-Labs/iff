The only text is exact lowercase 'iff'. TYPOGRAPHIC DIRECTION: a sophisticated bespoke lowercase iff wordmark drawn as an unconventional editorial ligature. Not rounded, not bubbly, not a stock Times Roman font. No separate symbol; the wordmark itself is the identity. Compact beautiful typographic composition.
