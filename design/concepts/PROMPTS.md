Avoid generic AI sparkle icons, giant hero sections, gradients, black chat sidebars, dollar-sign decorations, unreadable microtext and overflowing layouts. This is a design concept, use only fictional data. SCREEN: DISCOVERIES. Main heading 'Your life, connected.' and a quiet subtitle 'A clearer picture for you. A place to start for your people.' Upper right primary button 'Add records'. Main layout: three concise top metrics '9 discoveries', '5 need your input', '2 ready to hand over'. Below them a quiet sage callout with a small question icon, text 'Does someone depend on this transfer?' and action 'Add context'. This is a beautiful practical workspace, not a landing page.
